MineVO Review 2026: A Browser Mining Simulator Paying in EVO

Category: Mining Simulator

Unproven

MineVO calls itself 'the most advanced browser-based blockchain simulator' where you manage virtual mining hardware and 'earn real rewards in a server-authoritative economy'. Its own stats strip says 2.1 EH/s net hashrate, $13.15k total paid and 241 days online, and a payouts ticker shows withdrawals of 28, 50 and 155 'EVO'. It's free to start and shows casino and FaucetPay adverts. What we couldn't find is what EVO is, how it converts to money, the minimum, or who runs it. A simulator whose rewards are in a site token is an experiment, not an income.

How we reviewed it

We have not been paid by MineVO and this page has no referral link. Everything below comes from MineVO's public home page, checked on 2 November 2026. We have not signed up or withdrawn, so this review carries no score and no earnings claims. The screenshots are our own captures of the public pages.

What is MineVO?

MineVO (minevo.co) presents "Live Mainnet V3.0", a simulator where you manage "realistic hardware", optimise power use and earn rewards. The page title calls it a cloud mining simulator. It says it is built on a "server-authoritative Node.js backend with MongoDB", with "anti-cheat" and a "dynamic market" economy. You can sign in with Google and start for free. The page carries third-party adverts, including a casino offer and a FaucetPay ad slot.

The Stats

The site reports 2.1 EH/s of "net hashrate" (a simulated number), $13.15k total paid and 241 days online. $13,150 over 241 days is about $55 a day across the whole site, which is small. The ticker lists withdrawals as "28.00 EVO", "155.00 EVO" and "50.00 EVO", and doesn't say what an EVO is worth. Don't read the dollar total as a promise to you: it's the site's own number, and "total paid" may include payouts in its own unit. Our real hourly rate guide shows how to value time spent on small earners.

What We Couldn't Verify

  • What EVO is, whether it trades anywhere and how it converts to dollars.
  • The minimum withdrawal, fees and payout methods.
  • Whether any purchase or deposit is needed to progress. The page says "start mining free" and doesn't mention deposits.
  • The operator and country, and independent reviews. We found none.

The Adverts

The page shows a casino promotion ("up to 5 BTC") and a FaucetPay ad slot. Third-party ads pay the site, which is normal, but click carefully: they are not part of the site's rewards.

Tips if You Try It

  1. Don't buy anything in the game until you've found the EVO value and the minimum.
  2. Use a throwaway Google account.
  3. Ignore the casino ads.
  4. Time one session and note the EVO earned.

Final Verdict

Verdict: unproven, an experiment

MineVO is a free browser game with a payout ticker in its own token and no stated exchange rate. Play it for fun if you like the format, but until it shows what EVO is worth and a verified withdrawal, don't count it as earnings or pay for upgrades.
