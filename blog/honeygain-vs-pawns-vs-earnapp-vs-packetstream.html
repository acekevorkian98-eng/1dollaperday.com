Privacy: What You Should Know Before Installing

These apps are legitimate and safe for home use — but you should understand what you're signing up for before installing anything.

What These Apps Actually Do

Businesses route traffic through your residential IP address for web scraping, price monitoring, and market research. From their client's perspective, the requests look like they come from a normal home user. That's exactly why your IP has value — and why they pay you for it.

All four apps claim to filter out illegal or malicious traffic and only allow legitimate business use. The beermoney community has been using these apps for years without widespread issues — but you're trusting each platform to enforce their own rules.

  • Check your ISP's terms of service — some ISPs technically prohibit reselling bandwidth. Most don't actively enforce this, but it's worth knowing.
  • Use on home connections only — don't run these on work networks, mobile data plans with caps, or shared networks without permission.
  • Don't use on metered connections — if you pay per GB, these apps will cost you money rather than make you money.

For most people on home unlimited broadband, the risk is minimal and the setup is standard practice in the passive income community. Just go in informed.

Tips to Maximize Your Earnings

  1. Run all 4 simultaneously. This is the single biggest lever. Each app connects to a separate network. Stack them.
  2. Use always-on devices. A desktop PC, a NAS drive, or a Raspberry Pi that never sleeps earns far more than a laptop you close at night.
  3. Run on multiple devices. Old phones (plugged in), spare laptops, unused desktops — each device multiplies your earnings across all four apps.
  4. Prioritize your best IP location. US and Western European IPs command the highest rates. If you have access to a US connection, prioritize it.
  5. Use Honeygain's JumpTask mode. It removes the $20 barrier, eliminates fees, and often gives a +10% bonus. Switch immediately.
  6. Refer others. Honeygain, Pawns.app, and EarnApp all have referral programs. Even a handful of referrals meaningfully boosts your monthly income.
  7. Check in monthly, not daily. There's nothing to do day-to-day. Set them up and check in once a month to collect payouts.
