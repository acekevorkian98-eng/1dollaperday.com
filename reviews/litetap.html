LiteTap Review 2026: An Hourly Litecoin Faucet Built Around Games

Category: Crypto Faucet

Use Caution

LiteTap offers a free Litecoin claim every hour, plus surveys, 'provably fair' slot-style games and referral commissions. It doesn't state the claim size or the minimum withdrawal on its public page, says faucet payouts 'follow the level system', and asks you to wager to rank up. The referral structure (50% of faucet claims, a percentage of game wagers, 5% of surveys, from Bronze level) matches the one on FreeTrump, which we reviewed, so it looks like the same style of site, though we can't confirm a shared operator. Its payouts and FAQ pages wouldn't load for us. Keep away from the games.

We have not been paid by LiteTap and this page has no referral link. Everything below comes from LiteTap's public home page, checked on 2 November 2026. What is LiteTap?

LiteTap (litetap.io) pitches itself as "tap into free Litecoin every hour". You claim from a faucet on a 60-minute timer, can complete partner surveys, play "provably fair" games and earn commissions from friends. Your faucet level, and so the payout per claim, depends on how much you wager. The home page's demo panel shows a slot machine with a "BET" button and a guest balance of 0.0820 LTC. That balance is a mock-up, not an earning.

What the Faucet Pays

The page shows a 60-minute countdown but never states the claim amount. It says payouts "follow the level system" and that you must "wager to rank up". That's the same arrangement we described in our FreeTrump review: the biggest faucet rewards sit behind gambling, while a base-level claim is tiny. With Litecoin at about $55 (CoinGecko, 2 November 2026), even a fraction of a litoshi-sized claim is a fraction of a cent, so the real question is how many hours it takes to reach the minimum, which isn't published.

Games and Referrals

  • Games: slot-style, "provably fair", with weekly cashback on net losses and contests. Odds and house edge aren't shown on the page we could read.
  • Referrals: the home page says 50% of faucet claims, 0 to 4% of game wagers and 5% of survey earnings. The FAQ reportedly gives different figures (0.4% of game profit, 5% of gift card value), and commissions apply once referrals reach Bronze level.
  • Pay claim: the FAQ says referral commissions are paid "out of our pocket", which we can't verify.

The games are gambling

Weekly cashback on net losses and wagering-based levels are casino mechanics. We don't recommend them on a $1-a-day budget.

Verdict: unproven, with gambling at its core

LiteTap is an hourly Litecoin faucet whose best rewards are tied to wagering and whose key numbers aren't published. Without a visible claim size, minimum or operator, we can't recommend more than a small, game-free test.
