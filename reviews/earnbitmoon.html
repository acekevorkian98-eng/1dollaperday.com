What is EarnBitMoon?

EarnBitMoon (earnbitmoon.club) is a cryptocurrency reward platform — a faucet and GPT (Get-Paid-To) site in one. You complete small tasks, accumulate internal Coins with a fixed USD value, and convert them to cryptocurrency when you withdraw.

It's been running long enough to build a track record, and its 2 million+ monthly visitors put it firmly in the established tier of the faucet world. Unlike the single-button faucets of the early 2010s, EarnBitMoon layers in PTC ads, shortlinks, offerwalls, daily bonuses, challenges, and a leveling system — making it a fuller earning ecosystem than most.

The entry barrier is zero. No deposit, no skill requirements. You show up, complete tasks, and the Coins add up. For active earners willing to work the offerwalls, this is one of the more practical free-crypto options available.

How EarnBitMoon Works

Getting Started

  1. Sign up: Visit earnbitmoon.club, register with an email and password, solve a captcha.
  2. Verify your email: Click the confirmation link before anything else — withdrawals require a verified account.
  3. Connect FaucetPay: Set up a free FaucetPay account and link it in EarnBitMoon's payout settings. This unlocks the lowest withdrawal threshold and fastest payouts.
  4. Start earning: The faucet is the first thing you see on the dashboard. Hit Roll, solve the captcha, collect Coins. Then work through PTC ads and offerwalls.

Earning Methods

  • 5-Minute Faucet: Roll every 5 minutes for a random Coin payout. Low per-claim value but compounds quickly with consistent use. Keep a pinned tab open.
  • PTC Ads: Click an ad, wait 5–40 seconds, solve a captcha. Guaranteed payout every time — no disqualification risk. Clear the daily queue in under 5 minutes.
  • Shortlinks: Navigate through ad-heavy intermediary pages for higher payouts. More frustrating than PTC, but pays more per click.
  • Offerwalls (CPX Research, BitLabs, TimeWall, CPALead): The real earner. A 15-minute survey on CPX Research can pay $0.50–$1.50. Two completed surveys a day and you've hit $1 before touching anything else.
  • Daily Bonus & Challenges: Log in every day to maintain a streak bonus. Complete activity targets (20 faucet rolls, 10 PTC ads) to claim bonus Coins from the Challenges tab.

Skip the Multiplier

EarnBitMoon has a coin flip and multiplier feature. Avoid it. The house edge will erase your earned Coins over time. Protect your balance — withdraw it instead.

Payment Information

FaucetPay (Recommended)

The fastest and lowest-threshold option. Minimum withdrawal of ~$0.20, processed instantly or within a few hours. Supports BTC, LTC, DOGE, TRX, SOL, and USDT. If you're not already on FaucetPay, set it up first — it's the backbone of the faucet ecosystem.

Payeer

E-wallet supporting both crypto and fiat (USD/EUR/RUB). Minimum ~$0.20. Processed within 24–48 hours.

Direct Crypto Wallet

Withdraw straight to a hardware wallet or exchange (Binance, Coinbase, etc.). Minimum threshold is higher (~$5+) due to blockchain network fees, and processing can take several days. Use FaucetPay for regular withdrawals and consolidate to a main wallet periodically via Litecoin or Tron to minimize fees.

VPNs Are Banned

Using a VPN, proxy, or Tor on EarnBitMoon will get your account permanently banned and your balance forfeited. Don't do it.
