FomoEarn Review 2026: Paid to Watch Videos, With a $0.01 USDT Payout

Category: Watch-to-Earn

Unproven

FomoEarn pays you in USDT for watching listed short videos to the end in its official Android app. Withdrawals start at $0.01 on BEP-20 USDT with no fee, so you can test a payout on the first day. It doesn't publish what a video pays, and its own counters (102,424 users, $11,025 paid out) work out to about 11 cents per registered user. There's no named operator, withdrawals can be manually reviewed for up to 72 hours, and watch-to-earn sites are a category where platforms have tended to vanish. Test with the free account and withdraw early.

How we reviewed it

We have not been paid by FomoEarn and this page has no referral link. Everything below comes from FomoEarn's public home page and blog, checked on 2 November 2026. We have not signed up or withdrawn, so this review carries no score and no earnings claims. The screenshots are our own captures of the public pages.

What is FomoEarn?

FomoEarn (fomoearn.com) is a watch-to-earn site built around an official Android app. You watch short listed videos to the end and credit posts only after the timer finishes. Leaving early means the view doesn't count. The desktop site shows the account, but the tasks are built for Android. It also has coins from daily bonus tiers that you can spend on boosts or convert to account credit.

What It Pays

FomoEarn doesn't publish a per-video amount. Its blog says amounts "follow the task list, limits, and method rules", and the task list isn't shown to visitors. The only hard numbers are the counters: 102,424 registered users and $11,025 paid out. That is about 11 cents per registered user, which tells you how small the average payout is, though plenty of those users will have signed up and never withdrawn. Treat it as an extra, not an income, and check our real hourly rate guide before spending time on it.

The site says "no deposit, no fee", and a blog post says it takes no withdrawal fee, although the network's own fee still applies on your side. Withdrawals are processed automatically but can be manually reviewed for up to 72 hours, and a transfer to a wrong address can't be reversed. A $0.01 BEP-20 withdrawal is a cheap way to prove the route works, but a network fee can exceed the amount, so read our cash-out minimums and fees guide before you rely on a tiny withdrawal.

Rules and Referrals

  • One account per person, even across devices.
  • No VPN or IP-changing tools, which can lock the account.
  • Referral commissions are 50% (level 1), 10% (level 2) and 1% (level 3) of your referrals' credit.

The site also says third-party automation isn't used, while its app "advances listed videos" on its own. How that works isn't explained, so don't add your own macros.

What We Couldn't Verify

  • No company name, owner, address or launch date, only a 2026 copyright line.
  • Live counters can't be independently checked.
  • The criteria for manual withdrawal review aren't stated.
  • No independent reviews or payment proofs in our search.

Tips if You Try It

  1. Do one day of videos and write down the actual credit per view.
  2. Request the $0.01 USDT withdrawal to test the route, and check the address twice.
  3. Don't use a VPN or second account.
  4. Don't automate it yourself. The app already advances tasks.
  5. Withdraw regularly instead of letting a balance build.

Read our warning signs an earning site is about to stop paying, since watch-to-earn is a category where sites have vanished with little notice.

Final Verdict

Verdict: unproven, cheap to test

FomoEarn is free, pays out from a cent, and explains its payout process better than most. But it publishes no rates, names no operator and has no outside track record. Run the test, withdraw the first cents, and don't commit real time until the payout has landed.
