What is PayUp.video?

PayUp.video is a video ad platform that pays out via FaucetPay for watching short advertisements. The pitch is simple: advertisers pay PayUp to get their ads seen, and PayUp passes a slice of that revenue to you. No surveys that disqualify you halfway through, no confusing point conversions, no waiting weeks for approval. You sign up, you watch, you earn.

What separates PayUp.video from the crowded video-ad field is the combination of extremely short videos (10-20 seconds), very low withdrawal thresholds, and a legitimate referral program. More importantly, it is fully automatable using a MacroDroid macro on Android — which turns it from an active task into a genuinely passive income stream running on a spare device while you do something else.

After running it for several weeks, it has earned a permanent spot in the daily stack here at $1perday.

How PayUp.video Works

The Core Earning Loop

The flow is as straightforward as it gets. Log in, and the dashboard immediately presents a queue of video ads ready to watch. Each ad runs for 10-20 seconds. When it finishes, you advance to the next one. Your balance updates after each view and pays out to FaucetPay. That is the entire loop — no captchas between videos, no focus checks, no disqualifications.

Why Short Videos Matter

The 10-20 second length is not a small detail. On platforms with 30-60 second ads, the time-per-dollar ratio becomes painful quickly. PayUp's short format means the macro cycling through videos is not burning minutes per loop — it is burning seconds. At scale across multiple devices, that difference compounds.

Automation with MacroDroid

PayUp.video is fully automatable on Android using a MacroDroid macro. The macro handles the entire loop: loading videos, waiting for completion, tapping to advance, and restarting if something goes wrong. Once set up, it runs indefinitely without manual input — making this a true set-and-forget earner. Run it on a spare Android device plugged into power and connected to Wi-Fi, and it earns around the clock.

The Referral Program

PayUp.video runs a multi-level referral system. Refer active users who watch videos and earn a percentage of their earnings indefinitely. As your network grows, so does your passive income. For anyone with an audience interested in micro-earning, this is a meaningful addition to the base earnings.

The referral math is straightforward: more active referrals = more passive earnings via FaucetPay. There is no cap on referrals and no minimum number required to participate.

The $1perday Strategy: How to Use This

  1. Spare Device Protocol: Run the MacroDroid macro on an old Android phone or tablet plugged into power and connected to Wi-Fi. Never sacrifice your primary device's battery or bandwidth for this.
  2. Set and Forget: Once the macro is running, check on it once a day. Restart if it has stalled. That is the entire management overhead.
  3. Stack with Other Automations: Run PayUp.video alongside LuckyWatch and MakeYouTask automations on the same or different devices. Multiple passive streams running concurrently is the whole point.
  4. Withdraw Frequently: Given the low minimum, do not let earnings sit. Withdraw to your FaucetPay account regularly rather than accumulating on-platform balance.
