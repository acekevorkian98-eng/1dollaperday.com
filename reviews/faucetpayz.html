How FaucetPayz Works

The Hourly Faucet

The faucet is the backbone of the daily routine. Set a timer for the claim interval, come back when it fires, click, collect. The payout is micro by definition — this is a faucet, not a lottery. The value is in the accumulation across dozens of daily claims. Running the faucet on a consistent schedule for a full month produces a meaningful FaucetPay balance when stacked alongside other earners.

PTC Ads

Paid-to-click ads pay per view. The rate is in the typical PTC range — fractions of a cent per ad, view duration measured in seconds. Ad inventory varies by region. Tier-1 countries tend to see more ads. Non-tier-1 regions may see thinner inventory on some days. Check back during different periods if ads are sparse — inventory tends to refresh throughout the day.

Shortlinks

Shortlink completions redirect you through a brief advertisement before delivering the destination URL. The process takes 5–15 seconds per link and pays more per click than PTC ads. Use these during active sessions when you have a few minutes to burn through the available inventory.

Offerwalls

Standard third-party offerwalls with app downloads, mobile game completions, and surveys. These are the same providers you will encounter across most faucet/PTC sites in this category. Pay varies significantly by offer type. App install offers typically pay more than surveys. Do not expect consistent offerwall inventory — treat it as a bonus layer for boosting your balance before a cashout, not as a reliable daily income source.
