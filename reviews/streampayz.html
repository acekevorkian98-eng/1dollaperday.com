What is StreamPayz?

StreamPayz operates in the same space as RewardVid: watch video content, earn cryptocurrency, withdraw to FaucetPay. The business model is identical across this category — advertisers pay for views, the platform takes a cut, you get the rest. What differentiates StreamPayz within that model is the stream format rather than short video clips, and a promo cards system that adds a bonus layer most users overlook entirely.

Unlike traditional video-ad-based earners where you watch 30-second ads on repeat, StreamPayz delivers longer stream content. That format change has implications for how you run it: streams are more amenable to truly passive operation because there are fewer transitions and fewer points where the platform might demand an attention check. If inventory is running, you can let it roll.

The faucet claim component gives you a second income stream from the same session. While the streams run, periodic faucet claims top up your balance. Two earning mechanisms, one open tab, one device. That is efficient.

How StreamPayz Works

Stream Watching

Navigate to the streams section, select a stream, and let it run. Earnings accumulate as the stream plays. Like other watch-to-earn platforms, there will be occasional attention check prompts — brief interruptions requiring a click to confirm you are still present. These vary in frequency. On a spare device within arm's reach, responding to them takes two seconds and does not interrupt your primary workflow.

Faucet Claims

The built-in faucet operates on a timer. Claim at each reset to stack additional crypto on top of stream earnings. Running both simultaneously is the correct approach — neither task interferes with the other, and your balance builds from two directions at once.

Attention Checks

StreamPayz, like all watch-to-earn platforms, uses attention checks to confirm human presence. You cannot set it running and walk away for hours. What you can do is position a spare device where clearing a prompt is a non-disruptive two-second action. That is the difference between "somewhat passive" and "genuinely useful background income."

The Spare Device Protocol

This is the same strategy used for RewardVid, adapted for StreamPayz. The principle does not change because the platform category is the same.

  1. Dig out the old phone: An old Android handset with a cracked screen that is otherwise functional is perfect for this. Plug it into power. Connect to Wi-Fi. Open StreamPayz in the browser.
  2. Start the streams and park the device: Place it within arm's reach. You do not need to look at it. You need to be able to tap it without breaking focus.
  3. Clear attention checks as they come: A tap every few minutes. That is the total active time cost.
  4. Claim the faucet on each reset: Set a phone alarm on your main device for the faucet interval. When it fires, reach over to the spare device, claim, go back to what you were doing.
  5. Check promo cards once per session: Visit the promo cards page at the start or end of each session. It takes 30 seconds and adds to your balance for free.
  6. Pair with RewardVid: If you have two spare devices, run StreamPayz on one and RewardVid on the other. Both feed FaucetPay. Both are watch-to-earn. The strategies are identical.
