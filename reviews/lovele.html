Lovele Review 2026: The Chat App That Pays Points, and What They're Worth

Category: Rewards App

Unproven

Lovele is two products in one app: a private, end-to-end encrypted chat, and a points program paid for by advertising. You earn points from its own games, partner apps, surveys, 10 to 30 second "Paid Views" and a daily spin, and can withdraw from "2 USD in points" to PayPal, gift cards, mobile top-up or crypto. What it doesn't publish is what a point is worth, so we can't tell you an hourly rate. The operator is a named company, but its payout rules sit behind pages we couldn't read. Worth a free look, with an eye on the in-app exchange rate.

How we reviewed it

We have not been paid by Lovele and this page has no referral link. Everything below comes from Lovele's public home page and "Ways to earn" page, checked on 2 November 2026. We have not signed up or withdrawn, so this review carries no score and no earnings claims. The screenshots are our own captures of the public pages.

What is Lovele?

Lovele leads with privacy: end-to-end encrypted messages, groups and calls, a friends-only feed, disappearing media and no password. Its security page says messages use the MLS standard (RFC 9420) with post-quantum hybrid keys generated on your device. We haven't reviewed its whitepaper, so treat the security claims as the company's own.

Ways to Earn Points

  • Tasks: add friends, chat, invite someone, or post a TikTok about Lovele (listed at 10,000 points).
  • Games: its own multiplayer game Rush, plus partner games. Rush pays 5, 4, 3 and 1 points for the top finishers and a daily top prize of 2,500 points.
  • App installs: try shopping, fitness, music or photo apps. Points arrive once the partner confirms.
  • Surveys: 3 to 10 minutes each, listed at 700 to 2,300 points.
  • Paid Views: 10 to 30 second page visits, listed at 40 to 120 points.
  • Daily wheel: a daily spin with prizes from 10 to 1,000 points.

The headline says "hundreds of ways to earn", but the page describes six categories. The sample balance of 18,450 points and the 12-day streak shown on the page are illustrations.

What a Point Is Worth

This is the gap that matters. Withdrawals start "from 2 USD in points", but the public pages don't say how many points make a dollar, so a 700-point survey could be worth pennies or a dollar. Until you see the rate in the app, you can't work out an hourly figure. When you test it, note the points-to-dollars rate before your first survey, then time one survey and one paid view. Our real hourly rate guide shows how to count setup and screening time.

Cashing Out

  • Methods: PayPal, gift cards, mobile top-up and crypto. Which ones you see depends on your country and on whether you use the app or the website.
  • Review: each withdrawal request is reviewed, then sent automatically.
  • Fees, age limit and country list: not stated on the public pages.

Privacy: The Ad Trade-Off

An encrypted chat app that also runs offerwalls is an unusual mix. Lovele says necessary cookies handle sign-in and security, and that analytics use Google Analytics without recording your name. When earning is on, partner offerwalls and ad banners set their own third-party cookies, and the page implies an advertising ID may be shared. You can switch these off, but that closes the offerwalls. Decide whether you're comfortable with trackers in exchange for points, and read the points terms before you sign up.

Pros and Cons

Pros

  • Free to join, with a named operating company
  • Low 2 USD withdrawal start
  • PayPal, gift card and crypto options
  • Several earning types, including short paid views
  • Cookie settings let you reject non-essential tracking

Cons

  • No points-to-money rate on the public pages
  • Fees, age limit and countries not stated
  • Install and survey points depend on partner confirmation
  • Rewards push you to invite and post about the app
  • Earning loads third-party trackers
  • No payment proofs or independent reviews found

Tips if You Try It

  1. Find the points rate first. Don't spend time until you know what 1,000 points pays.
  2. Read the points terms and check which payout methods your country gets.
  3. Reject non-essential cookies if you only want the chat.
  4. Skip the invite and post tasks unless you're comfortable sharing.
  5. Reach 2 USD and cash out before you decide whether it's worth a daily habit.

Final Verdict

Verdict: unproven, check the points rate before you invest time

Lovele has a named company, a low cash-out start and a good spread of earning types, but without a published exchange rate or any payment proof we can't say what it pays. Try it free, learn the rate, and treat it as a side extra only if the numbers work.
