How BUX.money Works

BUX.money offers four primary ways to earn - each with different time-to-reward ratios.

This is the classic PTC experience. You click a link, a timer counts down (usually 10 to 60 seconds), and you solve a simple visual captcha.

The Reality

The pay per click is very small (0.02-0.05₽). You're trading seconds for fractions of a cent. However, the surfing list is frequently replenished, meaning you rarely run out of ads to click.

2. YouTube Views

Similar to surfing, but you watch (or leave running) specific YouTube videos assigned by advertisers looking to boost their view counts.

The Reality

This is arguably the most passive way to earn on the site. If you have a secondary device or an extra tab, you can keep the "video faucet" running while you focus on other high-value tasks.

3. Tasks (The High-Value Choice)

Tasks are where the "real" money is. These include:

  • Signing up for other websites

  • Social media actions

A single task can pay 10x to 50x more than a single surf click (1₽ to 15₽). If you're serious about hitting the $1/day mark, you cannot ignore the Tasks section.

4. Browser Extension

BUX.money offers an extension that notifies you of new ads and occasionally displays banners in the corner of your browser.

The Reality

It's a "set and forget" tool that ensures you don't miss the higher-paying ads that expire quickly.
