PixelPointTV Network Review 2026: 900 Points a Dollar and Few Answers

Category: Watch-to-Earn

Unproven

PixelPointTV runs a family of near-identical video sites (Lolvault.tv, Cozycrafts.tv, Gamersden.tv, Nestmade.tv, Recipe Ninja, Puppy Bear and others) that promise to pay points for watching. The one hard number is on the home page: 900 points = $1.00. What it doesn't say is how many points a video is worth, what the minimum payout is, or how you get paid, and no company is named. Several pages show a Facebook sign-in that is 'temporarily unavailable' and a note that rewards are 'active on this account elsewhere'. With no payout terms, treat it as unproven.

What is PixelPointTV Network?

PixelPointTV is a network of themed video sites on the same template. The home pages list sister sites including TicTacTi.com, Cozycrafts.tv, Recipepanda.tv, Puppybear.tv, Gamersden.tv, Recipeninja.tv, Nestmade.tv, Lolvault.tv, Globeset.tv, Vlogaday.com, Quickrecipes.tv, Discoverden.tv and Discoverhub.tv. Each carries a headline of "Want to earn free cash within minutes?", a video library ("10000+ videos"), featured creator channels, about 25 browser games with "now playing" counts, and an Advertise link. The footer points to PixelPointTV for support and legal pages.

What We Can Work Out

The only figure is "For each video you watch, you can earn points. 900 points = $1.00." The pages don't say how many points a video earns, whether there's a daily cap, or whether the games earn points. So we can't calculate an hourly rate, and neither can you until you've watched a video while signed in and seen the credit. Because almost all watch-to-earn models cost more in advertiser money than they pay out only when volume is high, expect small amounts. Things That Look Off

  • No payout terms. No minimum payout, payment method, schedule or fees on the pages we could read.
  • Odd notices. A "rewards active on this account elsewhere" message, and a Facebook login marked "temporarily unavailable" with prompts to reset your password.
  • Inconsistent view counts. Some recent videos show zero views while "most viewed" items show over a million, and long runs of near-identical titles look like filler.
  • Self-reported counters. "425+ sign-ups in the past 24 hours" can't be checked.
  • Template network. A dozen identical sites suggest one operator, though the pages don't confirm it.

What We Couldn't Verify

  • Any payout, minimum or payment method.
  • The operator and its location. The footer names only PixelPointTV and links Terms, DMCA, Privacy, "Do not sell my information" and "Delete account".
  • Independent reviews. We didn't find any for the network.

Pros and Cons

Pros

  • Free to join
  • Clear 900 points = $1.00 rate
  • Has Terms, DMCA, privacy and account-deletion links
  • Large library and browser games

Cons

  • Points per video, minimum and payout method not published
  • No named operator
  • Login notices that look unfinished
  • Filler-looking content and inconsistent view counts
  • Thirteen near-identical sister sites, unclear relationship

Tips if You Try It

  1. Sign up with a throwaway email and don't reuse a password.
  2. Watch one video and check the credit before spending time.
  3. Find the payout terms in your account before you watch more.
  4. Don't sign in with Facebook if prompted to reset passwords.
  5. Never pay or enter card details.

Final Verdict

Verdict: unproven, no payout terms to judge

PixelPointTV states a points rate and nothing else that matters: no points per video, no minimum, no payout method, no operator. Until its account pages show real payouts, treat it as a curiosity and put your time into platforms that publish their terms.
