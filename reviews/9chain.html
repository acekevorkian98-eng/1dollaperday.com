9Chain Community Review 2026: LOVE9 Points and the Simulated Node

Category: Points Farm

No Payout Yet

9Chain Community is not a way to earn money today. It's a points game attached to a blockchain project: you start a "simulated" node in your browser, run quests and invite friends to collect LOVE9 Points. The site says plainly that the points are not assets, can't be transferred and can't be converted inside the app, and that any future use would need a formal proposal and community vote. It is free and asks for no deposit, but it names no operator and its points have no defined value. Spend ten minutes a day at most, and only if you're happy to farm points for a possible token that may never arrive.

How we reviewed it

We have not been paid by 9Chain and this page has no referral link. Everything below comes from the 9Chain Community page and FAQ and one outside listing, checked on 2 November 2026. We have not signed up, so this review carries no score and no earnings claims. The screenshots are our own captures of the public page, and the member and node counts shown there are the site's own figures.

What is 9Chain Community?

9Chain describes the site (9chain.com) as the community hub of its ecosystem, separate from the network itself and from the official project site (9chain.org). It says it is in a pilot phase. The homepage claims 192,772 members from 205 countries and 143,987 simulated nodes started.

How It Works

  • Simulated node. It runs in your browser or on your phone, and the FAQ says it is not a validator. It earns LOVE9 Points every hour and on each "PUSH" (a term the page doesn't define).
  • Quests. Short chains of tasks, each with a points reward.
  • Upgrades. Node tiers are paid for with LOVE9 Points. The page also says tiers are "bought separately" without explaining what that means.
  • Voting. Members can vote on how the project develops.

What the Points Are Worth

According to the site's FAQ, LOVE9 Points are not assets, not transferable, not LOVE9 Coin and not convertible inside the app. Any distribution terms "would be set only after a formal proposal and a community vote." No withdrawal process and no token launch are described on the page.

An airdrop-listing site, CoinGabbar, describes a $LOVE9 token with a 9 billion supply and 30% set aside for the community, and says tasks include joining Telegram and X and submitting a wallet address. It also says the team has not shared much information and that its dates can't be checked. We couldn't verify those figures, and we found no exchange listing or audit. In short, the points are a bet on something that doesn't yet exist.

The Referral Layers

You earn 20% of your direct invitees' points and 10% of their invitees', paid from a "programme fund", and you must start your own node first. Multi-level bonuses are normal in points farms, but they reward recruiting more than doing. Don't let them drive you to bring in friends for something with no cash value yet.

Pros and Cons

Pros

  • Free, with no hardware or deposit
  • Only about ten minutes a day, per the site
  • Honest FAQ: it says plainly that points are not assets
  • Links an on-chain explorer for the project's testnet

Cons

  • No cash-out and no defined point value
  • Operator not named
  • Multi-level referral bonuses
  • Some tiers are "bought separately"
  • Not available in the US, UK, EEA or Switzerland
  • Airdrop listings warn the team is hidden

Tips if You Try It

  1. Never pay for a node tier. The site itself says points have no cash value.
  2. Never send money or crypto to anyone selling "community tokens". The page warns they aren't telling the truth.
  3. Don't connect a wallet you hold funds in. Use a fresh one if one is requested.
  4. Cap your time. Ten minutes a day is the whole commitment.

Our warning signs an earning site is about to stop paying applies to any points project with no cash-out.

Final Verdict

Verdict: speculative, with no payout today

9Chain Community costs nothing, and its FAQ is upfront that the points have no value yet. That is also the problem: you are working for something that may never pay. If you want money this week, use the platforms below.
