Everve Review 2026: A Task Platform Moving to TaskPulse, With No Dates Given

Category: Task Platform (Migrating)

Moving to TaskPulse

Everve, a task platform for performers and advertisers, no longer shows a normal site. Its home page is a single notice, 'Everve is moving to TaskPulse', saying it is consolidating into TaskPulse and that you should sign in with the Google account you used before. It gives no migration dates, no word on balances, withdrawals or payouts, and no support contact. TaskPulse's own site was in maintenance mode when we first looked and returned an access-forbidden error on our last check. So there's nothing here to review as an earner. If you have an Everve balance, withdraw before you do anything else, if you still can.

How we reviewed it

We have not been paid by Everve and this page has no referral link. Everything below comes from Everve's current single-page notice and a check of the TaskPulse site, checked on 2 November 2026. We have not signed up or withdrawn, so this review carries no score and no earnings claims. The screenshots are our own captures of the public pages.

What is Everve?

Everve (everve.net) presented itself as a platform with tasks for performers, campaigns for advertisers and a referral program. Today its home page is a transition notice titled "Platform transition in progress", with a language selector and a Google sign-in box. It says Everve is "being moved into TaskPulse so future activity is handled in one place", that you can still reach your account by signing in with Google, and that "existing referral access remains supported during the transition".

What the Notice Doesn't Say

  • When the move starts or ends, or when Everve closes.
  • What happens to existing balances, pending tasks or withdrawals.
  • Whether TaskPulse pays the same rates, minimums or methods.
  • Who runs either platform, or how to contact support.

The page also says "access to TaskPulse currently starts after authorization", which means you must sign in before you can see anything about the new platform.

What We Found at TaskPulse

When we first checked taskpulse.io on 2 November 2026 it showed a "maintenance mode" page, and a later check returned an access-forbidden error. We couldn't see what the new platform offers, so we can't review it. If a public TaskPulse site appears, we'll review it on its own terms.

If You Have an Everve Account

  1. Check your balance and withdraw it if the site still lets you, before migrating.
  2. Only sign in from the everve.net address you used before, and check the address bar. A look-alike migration page is an easy phishing trick.
  3. Don't give new details (ID, bank or card) to complete a migration without a written explanation.
  4. Screenshot your balance and history in case of a dispute.

Read our warning signs an earning site is about to stop paying: a forced move to a new platform with no dates is one of them, though not proof of a problem on its own.

Pros and Cons

Pros

  • Existing users are told they can still sign in
  • Referral access is said to be supported during the move
  • A clear notice instead of a silent shutdown

Cons

  • No dates, balances or payout information
  • No operator or support contact on the page
  • New platform not publicly viewable when we checked
  • Access requires Google sign-in first

Tips if You Try It

  1. Withdraw any balance first.
  2. Check the address bar before signing in with Google.
  3. Don't recruit new referrals until the terms are clear.
  4. Keep records of your earnings.

Final Verdict

Verdict: nothing to rate, protect your balance

Everve is in the middle of a move that it hasn't explained, and the destination wasn't viewable. There is no earning proposition to review right now. If you hold a balance, withdraw it before migrating, and watch for a public TaskPulse site before you put time in.
