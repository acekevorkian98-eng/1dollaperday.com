Acashly Review 2026: A GPT and PTC Site That Paid $718 to 3,452 Members, Now Unreachable

Category: GPT and PTC (Offline)

Offline

Acashly (acashly.com) was a get-paid-to and PTC platform: offerwalls and surveys, PTC ads, shortlinks, a faucet, daily tasks and contests, paid out through FaucetPay, Litecoin, Cwallet and USDT with a $1 minimum. The Internet Archive's last copy, from 28 April 2026, showed 3,452 members, 11,636 offers done and $718.64 paid in total, about 21 cents per member. On 2 November 2026 the domain no longer resolves at all. We found no closure notice. If you had a balance there, you can't withdraw it now, so keep your records.

How we reviewed it

We have not been paid by Acashly and this page has no referral link. We have not signed up or withdrawn, so this review carries no score and no earnings claims. The screenshots are our own captures of the public pages.

What is Acashly?

Acashly described itself as a "trusted global GPT and PTC platform" where users earn from surveys, offerwall tasks, PTC ads and daily activities. The menu listed View Advertisement, Offerwalls and Surveys, ZerAds PTC Surf, Shortlink Ads and a Faucet Claim page, plus an Advertise section, a forum, contests and a leaderboard. The footer says it was "powered by Kfscript", a ready-made PTC script, which means many near-identical sites run on the same software.

The Numbers It Showed

  • Members: 3,452 (with 1 joined "today").
  • Offers done: 11,636.
  • Total paid: $718.64, which works out to about 21 cents per member, and about 6 cents per completed offer.
  • Minimum withdrawal: $1.00, with processing "from 10 minutes to 24 hours" and "zero hidden fees".
  • Methods: FaucetPay, Litecoin, Cwallet and USDT (ERC20).

Those are small totals for a site billing itself "trusted global", and they show how little a typical member took out. Our real hourly rate guide explains why numbers like this are normal for the category.

Referral Rates

  • 30% of your referrals' PTC clicks.
  • 25% of their GPT offers.
  • 20% of their advertising purchases or upgrades.
  • 15% of their daily tasks.

Those are generous on paper. They're paid out of the same small pool, so they won't add up to much.

Where It Is Now

On 2 November 2026 acashly.com doesn't resolve in DNS, which means the domain is no longer pointing anywhere. The archive first saved it in July 2025 and last saved it on 28 April 2026. We found no closure notice, no payout plan and no operator name. If you earned a balance, we can't tell you how to recover it, and we'd avoid anyone offering to. See our warning signs guide.
