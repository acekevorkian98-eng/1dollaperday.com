FireFaucet v5: Cloud Auto-Faucet, Full Redesign & What Changed

News · 6 min read

FireFaucet v5 is a ground-up rebuild of the platform. The headline change: the auto faucet now runs in the cloud — no more keeping a tab open. There's a full site redesign, a new Fuel system that self-recharges, rebuilt Daily Tasks, redesigned Levels & Rewards, and two new currencies (SOL & USDC). The 8th Anniversary event starts July 25th. If you've been waiting for a reason to try (or return to) FireFaucet, this is it.

What Is FireFaucet v5?

FireFaucet has been running since 2018 — making it one of the oldest auto faucets still paying consistently. But for a long time, the core experience stayed roughly the same: earn ACP, keep a tab open, watch it tick. v5 changes that fundamentally.

The v5 update launched on July 1, 2026, and it's the biggest overhaul the platform has seen since v4.0 dropped back in 2020. A follow-up v5.1 landed two weeks later with additional features. Together, they represent a genuine rethinking of how the platform works.

We've been using FireFaucet for years (our full review covers the pre-v5 experience in detail), so this update was worth a proper look.

The Big One: Cloud Auto-Faucet

This is the change that matters most. Previously, the auto faucet required you to keep a browser tab open on a dedicated device. That meant burning a tab, burning bandwidth, and hoping your computer didn't go to sleep mid-session.

v5 replaces all of that with a cloud-based auto faucet. It runs on FireFaucet's servers, not in your browser. You turn it on, and it keeps claiming in the background — even when your laptop is closed.

This Is a Genuine Upgrade

The cloud auto faucet alone justifies the update. For anyone who was running FireFaucet alongside other tasks or as a background earner, not needing a dedicated tab is a massive quality-of-life improvement. It turns FireFaucet from "something I have to babysit" into "something that just runs."

The cloud faucet is powered by a new resource called Fuel. Fuel self-recharges in the background, up to a maximum capacity determined by your level. Higher levels mean faster recharge rates and larger Fuel tanks. This creates a natural progression incentive — the more you use the platform, the more efficiently the auto faucet runs.

The Fuel System Explained

Fuel replaces the old "spend ACP to auto-claim" model. Here's how it works now:

  • Base Fuel — self-recharges automatically, rate scales with your level
  • Bonus Fuel — earned from Daily Tasks, the Faucet, and the Daily Bonus (no cap, separate from base Fuel)
  • Fuel overflow protection — when your Bonus Fuel tank is full (minimum 1,000 capacity, grows with level), excess converts to Activity Points at 1:1

The daily bonus Fuel was bumped from 10 to 25 in v5.1. The recharge ladder was also reshaped: you now gain +1 fuel/hour every 20 levels (previously every 50), capping at 50/hour at Level 800 instead of Level 1000.

There's also a new live earnings widget at the bottom of every page while the auto faucet is running. It shows your earnings in real time and lets you stop and collect anytime. Small feature, but it removes the uncertainty of "is it actually working?"

The Full Site Redesign

The visual overhaul is immediately noticeable. FireFaucet v5 has a cleaner interface, better responsiveness across desktop, tablet, and mobile, and a more polished overall feel. Navigation has been reorganised — earning sections are easier to find, your balance and Fuel status are always visible, and the dashboard gives a better at-a-glance view of your account.

The old design was functional but increasingly dated. The new one feels like it belongs in 2026. If you bounced off FireFaucet before because the UI felt cluttered or confusing, it's worth another look.

Other visual changes include:

  • New custom icons for ACP, Fuel, Bonus Multiplier, and Activity Points
  • Level bonus now shown as a clear percentage instead of a multiplier
  • Redesigned Levels & Rewards page with the ability to preview upcoming rewards
  • Per-currency ACP costs — each coin now has its own cost per claim based on its value

Rebuilt Daily Tasks & Power Streak

Daily Tasks have been redone from the ground up. The goals are clearer, the rewards are bigger, and completing them now grants Bonus Fuel on top of the usual ACP and EXP.

v5.1 added Power Streak — complete 10,000 ACP worth of offerwalls each day to keep a streak alive. The daily reward grows with your streak, up to 2,000 ACP per day. This is a meaningful incentive for daily active users, and the team has hinted at more perks tied to streaks in future updates.

New Currencies: SOL & USDC

Solana (SOL) and USD Coin (USDC on Solana) are now available for auto claim and withdrawals. Meanwhile, DASH and DGB are being retired on July 31st — existing balances will be auto-migrated, or you can exchange them for free before then.

What the Community Is Saying

The response has been mostly positive, with some caveats. Trustpilot reviews since the v5 launch note the improved design and the convenience of the cloud auto faucet. The most common praise: "The only upgrade is you can now autoclaim without keeping the site open."

The most common criticism: some users feel that earning rates on certain tasks were adjusted downward as part of the rework, and that the shift to Fuel (instead of direct ACP spending) changes the earning math in ways that aren't immediately obvious. This is worth monitoring — the team has a track record of tweaking rates post-launch based on feedback.

The 8th Anniversary event, announced for July 25th, should bring additional bonuses and give a clearer picture of where rates settle long-term.
