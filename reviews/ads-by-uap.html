ADS by UAP Review 2026: A Crypto PTC Network With Claims and No Rates

Category: PTC and Ad Network

Unproven

ADS by UAP calls itself 'the premier crypto advertising network'. Members earn by viewing campaigns, advertisers buy traffic, and payouts start at $0.05 through FaucetPay, crypto or USD. It claims 100,000+ active members and over $402,000 paid, and it sits inside PassiveTools, a hub of faucets, virtual-mining games and a rewards comparison page. None of those figures can be checked, no earning rate or advertiser price is shown, and the operator is not named. We found no independent reviews, and ads.uap.company itself didn't load for us. Unproven.

How we reviewed it

We have not been paid by ADS by UAP and this page has no referral link. Everything below comes from the ADS by UAP page on PassiveTools and a search that found no independent reviews, checked on 2 November 2026. We have not signed up or withdrawn, so this review carries no score and no earnings claims. The screenshots are our own captures of the public pages.

What is ADS by UAP?

ADS by UAP is described on passive.tools as a "high-impact PTC and traffic network": you earn "viewing high-yield campaigns", or you advertise affiliate links to its members with "guaranteed human visits, anti-cheat detection and competitive CPC rates". PassiveTools is a wider hub that says it has curated crypto earning tools "since 2010", and links to virtual mining and faucet games (Lightning Miner, TRX Miner, CryptoCrops, SatsMan) and a Rewards Hub comparison page. The network's own domain, ads.uap.company, did not resolve when we tested it.

The Claims

  • Members: 100,000+ active.
  • Paid out: over $402,000 to publishers and viewers.
  • Minimum withdrawal: $0.05.
  • Payout channels: FaucetPay, crypto, USD.

$402,000 over 100,000 members is about $4 each on the site's own figures, over a history that isn't dated, so it says little about what a new member earns. The page gives no pay-per-view figure, no ad counts and no advertiser price list. Our PTC sites roundup and real hourly rate guide show the scale to expect from PTC work.

The Ecosystem Around It

PassiveTools pushes a "daily earning stack": claim from seven free faucets and mining pools every day. Several of those are virtual-mining or play-to-earn games, which can involve purchases. This page mentions no deposits, but we couldn't read the terms of those products, so check each before paying for anything. A daily routine spread over seven small faucets also costs real time for cents.

What We Couldn't Verify

  • No legal entity, address, rules or terms on the page. Support is through Telegram and a DM account.
  • No referral rate: the page only says "generous referrals".
  • The member and payout figures.
  • Any independent review. A search found only reviews of a different network, A-ADS.

Pros and Cons

Pros

  • Free to join
  • Low $0.05 minimum withdrawal
  • FaucetPay, crypto and USD payouts
  • Part of a long-running hub that dates itself to 2010

Cons

  • No pay rates or advertiser prices
  • Claims can't be verified
  • No operator or terms visible
  • Network domain didn't load for us
  • Hub pushes many small faucets and mining games
  • No independent reviews

Tips if You Try It

  1. Sign up with a throwaway email and read the rules in the dashboard.
  2. Time ten ad views and calculate the pay per minute.
  3. Never buy mining packages or boosts from the hub.
  4. Test the $0.05 withdrawal to FaucetPay early.
  5. Advertisers: start with the smallest budget and measure the traffic.

See our warning signs an earning site is about to stop paying for what to monitor.

Final Verdict

Verdict: unproven, cheap to test, not worth much time yet

ADS by UAP has a low minimum and sensible payout channels, but its numbers are unverifiable, its terms are missing and its own domain didn't load. Try the free account, test one small withdrawal and decide from your own figures.
