BTCClicks Review 2026: The PTC Site That Has Been Offline Since February

Category: PTC (Offline)

Offline

BTCClicks was a Bitcoin paid-to-click site. As of 2 November 2026, btcclicks.com doesn't load: Cloudflare returns error 522, meaning the origin server is timing out. The Internet Archive's records show the site returning normal pages as late as 2 February 2026 and returning that same 522 from 17 February 2026 onward, so it has been down for about nine months. Nothing on the web that we could find announces a shutdown, a payout plan or a return. If you had a balance there, treat it as lost unless the operator reappears, and don't pay for anything claiming to recover it.

What is BTCClicks?

BTCClicks (btcclicks.com) was a PTC site where members viewed ads for small Bitcoin amounts, with a referral program and paid advertising packages. Older third-party reviews describe an earning ceiling of about 10 to 15 ads a day, a single payment method, and very high referral commissions (80% for standard members, 160% for premium), claims we couldn't check against the live site. Our own bookmark of its ads page no longer loads.

What the Evidence Shows

  • Live check, 2 November 2026: HTTP 522 "Connection timed out". Cloudflare is up, but the origin server behind btcclicks.com isn't answering.
  • Internet Archive: normal captures as recently as 2 February 2026, then 522 errors on 17 February 2026, which are the latest captures it holds.
  • Third-party reports: one PTC review site describes the site as closed, with an undated page we couldn't confirm.
  • Official word: we found no announcement, no closing notice and no payout plan.

A 522 can sometimes be a temporary hosting fault, but nine months of it, with no statement from the operator, is the pattern we describe in our guide to warning signs an earning site is about to stop paying.

If You Had a Balance

  • Check your email for any closure notice, and only trust one that comes from an address you know.
  • Do not pay a "recovery" service or share wallet seed phrases. Recovery scams target abandoned sites.
  • Keep your records in case the site returns.
  • Treat any BTCClicks look-alike domain as unsafe.
