Quick answers on earning small amounts online

The questions people ask most, answered from our 93 reviews. Picks are our highest scores with no active warnings, updated 10 November 2026.

How can I make a little money online without spending anything?

Stick to free sites that never ask for a deposit: paid surveys and research studies, offerwalls, crypto faucets, PTC sites and passive apps. Surveys and research pay the most per hour; faucets and PTC pay the least. Good places to start: Prolific (9.5/10) and Respondent (9.2/10) for surveys, Freecash (9.0/10) and GG2U (8.5/10) for offerwalls, and Honeygain (8/10) running in the background.

How much can you realistically earn from micro-earning?

Most people earn $0.50 to $2.00 an hour. Consistent effort across several sites usually adds up to $5 to $20 a month, and efficient stackers report $15 to $30 a month at best. It is pocket money, not an income. See the real hourly rate breakdown.

What are PTC sites, and do they actually pay?

PTC (paid to click) sites pay you to view ads, usually around $0.001 per ad, plus small extras like surveys and shortlinks. The established ones do pay, slowly. Our top-scored PTC sites: adBTC (8/10) and RewardJoy (formerly CoinPayU) (8.0/10). SnazzyPTC is our own stab at building the best possible PTC site, so we leave it unscored. Guide: PTC sites explained.

What is the best crypto faucet in 2026?

Our highest-scored faucets right now are FaucetCrypto (8.5/10), FaucetPay (8.5/10), Dutchy CORP (8.2/10) and EarnBitMoon (8/10). FireFaucet is our featured faucet: earn fuel with daily tasks and its cloud auto faucet claims for you. Full rankings: best faucets 2026.

How can I earn free crypto?

Crypto faucets and crypto PTC sites pay in crypto directly, usually into the FaucetPay micro-wallet or your own wallet. Some cash sites, such as Freecash, also let you cash out in crypto. Expect small amounts, and watch withdrawal minimums and network fees. See crypto faucets explained.

How do I make $1 a day online?

Combine a passive app running in the background, a daily faucet routine and a few surveys or offers. We lay out the exact free stack in how to make $1 a day online and the routine in our daily guide.

What pays the most for my time?

Paid research studies and surveys. Our top scores: Prolific (9.5/10), Respondent (9.2/10) and UserTesting (9.0/10). Offerwalls come next. PTC ads and faucets have the lowest hourly rates.

Can I earn money passively?

Yes, slowly. Bandwidth-sharing apps earn from your unused internet connection with no daily effort, typically a few dollars a month per device. Our top picks: Honeygain (8/10), Pawns.app (8.0/10) and EarnApp (7.8/10). See best passive income apps.

Do I need to pay or deposit anything to start?

No. Legitimate earning sites never need a deposit. If a site asks you to pay, buy a plan or deposit crypto to unlock earnings or withdrawals, treat it as a scam until proven otherwise.

Which wallet do I need for faucets and PTC sites?

Many faucets and PTC sites pay into FaucetPay, a free micro-wallet with very low minimums. Others, such as FaucetCrypto, now pay straight to your own crypto wallet. See the FaucetPay guide.

How do I spot a scam earning site?

Red flags: a deposit or paid plan to withdraw, promises like "$100 a day clicking ads", no payment proof from real users, very high minimum withdrawals, and earnings that depend on recruiting. See scam alerts and 7 warning signs a site is about to stop paying.
