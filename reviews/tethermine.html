TetherMine Review 2026: A USDT “Cloud Miner” Advertising Up to 100% a Day, Now Offline

Category: Cloud Mining (Offline)

Do Not Deposit

TetherMine (tethermine.online) described itself as a 'new USDT cloud mining platform' with '150% profits just 24 hour'. Its plan table, as saved by the Internet Archive on 22 July 2026, pays 50% a day on a $1 plan, 60% a day on $50, 90% a day on $1,000 and 100% a day on $2,000, for 30 days. The top plan turns $2,000 into a claimed $60,000. No real mining pays that. Its login also asked you to 'enter your FaucetPay credentials' with an email and password. On 2 November 2026 the site returns a host error. We haven't tested it and we recommend you don't deposit, and never type a FaucetPay password into any other site.

How we reviewed it

We have not been paid by TetherMine and this page has no referral link. Everything below comes from the Internet Archive's saved copy of tethermine.online from 22 July 2026 (the live site shows a host error), checked on 2 November 2026. We have not signed up or withdrawn, so this review carries no score and no earnings claims. The screenshots are our own captures of the public pages.

What is TetherMine?

The archived page offers a free "50 Gh/s" node on sign-up, nine paid plans from $1 to $2,000, a profit calculator, a "live network" panel of deposits and payouts, and a 25% referral commission on referrals' deposits. The sign-up form says "Enter your FaucetPay credentials to begin" and asks for a FaucetPay email, a "secure password" and a captcha, and "if you don't have an account, one will be created automatically". The footer names no company, only "New Premium USDT Miner", and claims the platform is "100% registered and secure".

The FaucetPay Credentials Form

The login box says "Enter your FaucetPay credentials to begin" and asks for a FaucetPay email and a "secure password". We can't tell whether that password is new to the site or is checked against FaucetPay, but a third-party site should never need your FaucetPay password. If you've typed one in, change it on FaucetPay now, and enable two-factor authentication.

Other Claims

  • Minimums: $1 USDT deposit and a 0.00001 USDT withdrawal, which are far below any network fee.
  • "No hidden fees", "100% registered and secure", "99.9% uptime": no registration, audit or company is shown.
  • Live network panel: the archived page shows zero users, deposits and payouts and "0 days online", and the "live" payouts rows were empty, so there is no public evidence of payouts.

Where It Is Now

On 2 November 2026 tethermine.online returns Cloudflare's "SSL handshake failed" (525) page, meaning the origin server isn't completing a secure connection. The Internet Archive first saved the site on 28 March 2026 and last on 22 July 2026.
