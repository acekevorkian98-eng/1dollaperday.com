Step 1: Set Up FaucetPay First

FireFaucet pays out to FaucetPay or directly to a wallet. FaucetPay is the easier route: it collects tiny payments from many faucets in one place, so a small FireFaucet withdrawal isn't eaten by network fees. If you don't have an account yet, create one first (FaucetPay, referral link) and read our FaucetPay review.

Step 2: Create Your FireFaucet Account

Sign up at FireFaucet and set FaucetPay as your withdrawal method. Use a unique password: micro-earning sites are a common target for credential stuffing.

Step 3: Pick Your Coins

Since v5, every coin has its own ACP cost per claim, based on its value. Pick one or two coins you're happy to hold and stick with them so your balance builds towards a withdrawal instead of being spread thin. v5 added Solana (SOL) and USDC on Solana; DASH and DigiByte were retired on 31 July 2026, so don't choose those.

Step 4: The 30-Minute Daily Routine

Everything you do on FireFaucet earns ACP (Auto Claim Points), and several tasks also earn Bonus Fuel for the auto faucet. In order of value for time:

  1. Daily Bonus. Claim it first. Since v5.1 it gives 25 Bonus Fuel.
  2. Manual faucet. A few minutes, and it adds Bonus Fuel too.
  3. Daily Tasks. Rebuilt in v5 with clearer goals. Completing them pays ACP, EXP and Bonus Fuel.
  4. Shortlinks. The ACP workhorse: typically 70 to 80+ links a day across several providers, around 110 to 130 ACP each, with a progress bonus for working through them in sequence. They reset at midnight.
  5. PTC ads. Low pay per click, but the supply never runs out. Good for filling spare minutes.
  6. Offerwalls (optional). If you complete 10,000 ACP worth of offerwalls in a day you keep a Power Streak alive, and its daily reward grows up to 2,000 ACP.

Step 5: Switch On the Cloud Auto Faucet

This is the part that makes FireFaucet worth it. The v5 auto faucet runs on FireFaucet's servers, not in your browser, so it keeps claiming when your laptop is closed. It runs on Fuel:

  • Base Fuel recharges on its own. You gain +1 Fuel per hour every 20 levels, up to 50 per hour at level 800.
  • Bonus Fuel comes from the Daily Bonus, the faucet and Daily Tasks. When the Bonus Fuel tank is full (at least 1,000, growing with level), the excess converts to Activity Points one-for-one, so nothing is wasted.
  • A live earnings widget at the bottom of the page shows what the auto faucet is making, and lets you stop and collect at any time.

Why levels matter

Your level sets how fast Fuel recharges and how big the tank is. The daily routine is what levels you up, so the first few weeks of consistency pay off for as long as you keep the account.

Step 6: Withdraw (Be Patient)

FireFaucet's one real weakness is a payout threshold that's higher than most faucets. Payments do come through, but the first withdrawal takes a while. Let the balance build, withdraw to FaucetPay, and don't chase the threshold by spreading across many coins.
